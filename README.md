---

## 🧠 Typical Writeup Flow

Most writeups follow this general format:

1. **Challenge Overview** – What the task is asking.
2. **Initial Analysis** – File, endpoint, or binary reconnaissance.
3. **Approach** – Step-by-step thought process.
4. **Exploitation / Solving** – Commands, scripts, or techniques used.
5. **Flag / Result** – Final output and verification.
6. **Key Takeaways** – What to remember for similar tasks.
